# Goblin Ambush

{{descriptive
The party crests the ridge and spots a band of goblins looting an
overturned wagon below.
}}

A pack of **goblins** has ambushed a merchant caravan on the old forest
road. If the party intervenes quickly, they can save the driver.

{{note
**Tip.** Goblins fight dirty — expect them to use the wagon and crates
as cover.
}}

{{monster,wide
## Goblin
*Small humanoid (goblinoid), neutral evil*
- **Armor Class** 15
- **Hit Points** 7 (2d6)
- **Speed** 30 ft.
}}

\column

## Tactics

If reduced to fewer than 2 hit points, a goblin will use its bonus action
to disengage and flee into the woods.

\page

# Aftermath

{{note
Once the goblins are dealt with, the driver, **Aldric Vane**, offers the
party 25 gold pieces and a ride to the next town.
}}

That's the end of the encounter.
